Best Gyros in Wembley

Greek-inspired street food meets Wembley flavour. Charcoal-grilled, hand-carved and wrapped in warm pita.

What Makes Our Gyros Special

Our gyros bring the best of Greek street food to Wembley. We slow-roast stacked layers of seasoned meat on a vertical spit, carving each portion to order so every bite is juicy and packed with flavour. The meat is nestled into warm, pillowy pita bread and topped with crisp salad, tangy tzatziki and our signature sauces.

Unlike mass-produced gyros, ours are made from whole cuts of premium halal chicken and lamb — never processed, never pre-formed. You can taste the difference in every bite.

Our Signature Gyros

Classic Chicken Gyro

Tender marinated chicken carved from the spit, wrapped in warm pita with fresh tomato, red onion, crisp lettuce and a generous drizzle of creamy tzatziki.

Lamb Gyro Deluxe

Slow-roasted seasoned lamb, hand-carved and piled into pita with grilled halloumi, pickled chillies, sumac onions and our house garlic sauce.

The Wembley Gyro Box

Your choice of chicken or lamb gyro meat served over seasoned rice with fresh salad, hummus, warm pita and all three of our signature sauces on the side.

Halloumi & Falafel Gyro

Our vegetarian option — crispy falafel and grilled halloumi in warm pita with hummus, tabbouleh, pickled turnip and a squeeze of lemon. Vegan option available without halloumi.

Fresh Ingredients, Bold Flavours

Our pita bread is baked fresh in-house throughout the day — soft, warm and slightly charred from the grill. Our tzatziki is made daily from thick Greek yoghurt, fresh cucumber, garlic and dill. Every salad is prepped each morning with locally sourced produce.

We also make our own chilli sauce, garlic mayo and a tangy pomegranate molasses dressing that pairs perfectly with lamb. All our meats are 100% halal certified.

Gyro Platters for Sharing

Feeding a group? Our gyro sharing platters are loaded with carved chicken and lamb, warm pita, rice, chips, grilled vegetables, hummus, tzatziki and all the sauces. Perfect for match days, family gatherings or a night out with friends.

We also offer kids' gyro wraps and meal deals that include a drink and side — great value for families visiting Wembley.
