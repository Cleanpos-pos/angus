What Makes Our Gyros Special

Our gyros bring the best of Greek street food to Wembley. We slow-roast stacked layers of seasoned meat on a vertical spit, carving each portion to order so every bite is juicy and packed with flavour. The meat is nestled into warm, pillowy pita bread and topped with crisp salad, tangy tzatziki and our signature sauces.

Unlike mass-produced gyros, ours are made from whole cuts of premium halal chicken and lamb — never processed, never pre-formed. You can taste the difference in every bite.

If you're searching for the best gyros in Wembley, our Greek-inspired wraps and platters deliver authentic flavours with a Wembley twist, all cooked fresh to order.

Fresh Ingredients, Bold Flavours

Our pita bread is baked fresh in-house throughout the day — soft, warm and slightly charred from the grill. Our tzatziki is made daily from thick Greek yoghurt, fresh cucumber, garlic and dill. Every salad is prepped each morning with locally sourced produce.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a gyro?

A gyro is a Greek street food made from seasoned meat (chicken or lamb) slow-roasted on a vertical spit, carved to order and served in warm pita bread with salad, tzatziki and sauces. It's similar to a doner kebab but with distinct Greek-Mediterranean seasoning and toppings.

Are your gyros halal?

Yes, all our meats are 100% halal certified. We also offer vegetarian (halloumi & falafel) and vegan options.

Can I get gyros delivered in Wembley?

Yes! We deliver across Wembley, Wembley Park, Alperton, Sudbury and surrounding areas. Visit our takeaways near me page for full delivery details.

Do you also serve burgers and kebabs?

Absolutely! We're famous for our charcoal-grilled burgers and authentic kebabs too. Everything is cooked over real charcoal.
