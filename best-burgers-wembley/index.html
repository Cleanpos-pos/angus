Best Burgers in Wembley

Welcome to Angus Burger — where quality meets taste. Charcoal-grilled, locally sourced, 100% halal.

Why Our Burgers Stand Out

Our chefs hand-press every patty and use only the finest locally sourced ingredients. We insist on freshness — there are no freezers or microwaves in our kitchen. From our grain-fed Scottish beef to the brioche buns baked daily, every element is crafted to ensure a juicy, flavour-packed bite.

We marinate, season and cook our meats over a smoky charcoal grill, delivering that authentic BBQ flavour Wembley loves. Vegetarians and vegans will love our plant-based range, and our halal and gluten-free options ensure everyone can enjoy a great burger.

Award-Winning Signature Burgers

The Wembley Deluxe

120-day grain-fed beef, melted cheese, crispy bacon and house sauce on a buttery brioche bun — our most popular burger, inspired by generous toppings and bold flavours.

Plant Power Smash

A juicy vegan patty with caramelised onions, vegan cheddar and our secret umami glaze — award-worthy plant-based goodness.

Smoky Londoner

Double beef patties with smoked cheddar, BBQ sauce, char-grilled halloumi and crispy onions — bold, smoky and unforgettable.

The Angus Dip

Slow-cooked beef brisket, pickles and melted Swiss cheese served with gravy for dipping — a signature Angus Burger original.

Fresh & Local Ingredients

We partner with local farmers and bakers to source seasonal produce and freshly baked buns. Our beef is Scottish and grain-fed, while our vegetables arrive daily from local markets. We use only halal-certified meats and offer vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free alternatives.

Our secret sauces and jams are made in-house. Every element is crafted from scratch — from our chilli dogs to thick shakes. Quality you can taste in every bite.

Customise Your Meal

Prefer extra toppings? We offer a wide array of extras: grilled mushrooms, caramelised onions, jalapeños, cheese varieties, sauces and pickles. Build your dream burger or keep it classic — it's up to you!

Don't forget to pair your burger with sides like our crunchy hand-cut fries, mac & cheese croquettes, or a refreshing milkshake with over ten mix-ins.

Visit Us Near Wembley Stadium

Find us at 7 Park Lane, Wembley, just a short walk from Wembley Stadium. Enjoy a relaxed dine-in experience or grab a takeaway before the game. Our spacious seating, friendly service and fast turnaround times make us ideal for families, friends and solo diners alike.
