Best Kebabs in Wembley

Authentic charcoal-grilled kebabs, marinated to perfection. 100% halal, made fresh daily in the heart of Wembley.

Why Wembley Loves Our Kebabs

At Angus Burger, our kebabs are anything but ordinary. Every skewer is hand-prepared using premium cuts of 100% halal lamb, chicken and kofte, marinated overnight in our signature blend of spices and herbs. We cook everything over a blazing charcoal grill, locking in smoky flavour and juicy tenderness that you simply can't get from a standard flat-top.

Whether you're after a classic doner, a flame-kissed shish or a loaded kebab wrap bursting with fresh salad and house-made sauces, we've got you covered. Our kebabs have earned us a loyal following across Wembley and beyond.

Our Signature Kebabs

The Charcoal Shish

Tender cubes of marinated lamb leg, grilled over charcoal until perfectly charred. Served on fluffy flatbread with grilled peppers, sumac onions and our garlic yoghurt sauce.

Chicken Adana

Hand-minced chicken blended with Aleppo pepper and fresh herbs, moulded onto flat skewers and charcoal-grilled. Spicy, smoky and incredibly moreish.

The Loaded Doner

Thinly sliced lamb doner piled high with crispy chips, fresh salad, pickled chillies and a drizzle of chilli and garlic sauce — Wembley's ultimate late-night feast.

Mixed Grill Platter

A sharing feast of lamb shish, chicken shish, kofte and doner with rice, flatbread, grilled veg, hummus and all the sauces. Perfect for families and groups.

Quality You Can Taste

We source our lamb from trusted UK farms and our chicken is always fresh, never frozen. All our meats are halal-certified and prepared on-site daily. Our marinades are made from scratch using traditional recipes — no shortcuts, no pre-made pastes.

Our flatbreads are baked fresh throughout the day, and our salads are prepped every morning with crisp lettuce, ripe tomatoes, crunchy red onion and fresh herbs. Pair your kebab with our hand-cut chips or fluffy rice for the complete experience.

Wraps, Boxes & Platters

Not just skewers — enjoy your kebab your way. Choose from warm wraps stuffed with meat and salad, kebab boxes with rice or chips, or go all-in with a sharing platter loaded with grilled meats, dips and sides.

We also offer vegetarian options including halloumi wraps, falafel boxes and grilled vegetable platters. Gluten-free flatbread is available on request.
